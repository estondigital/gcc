Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Growth Clarity Co. ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through growthclarityco.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and company details when you book a call, take the GTM diagnostic, or subscribe to the newsletter. We also collect basic usage data (pages visited, referral source) through standard analytics tools.

How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule calls
  • To generate and send your GTM diagnostic results
  • To send newsletter content you've explicitly subscribed to
  • To improve our website and service offerings

What we don't do

We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not share your diagnostic answers or contact details outside of the tools directly used to deliver our services (such as email delivery and scheduling software).

Cookies and analytics

This site uses standard analytics cookies to understand traffic patterns. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; this will not affect your ability to browse the site, though some interactive tools may not function correctly.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send.
