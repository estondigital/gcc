01

From disconnected signals to a decision brief.

What changed?
What could explain it?
What should we investigate next?

The intended product would help a SaaS team bring marketing, sales, product and customer-success evidence into a structured investigation. The emphasis is on traceable reasoning, evidence gaps and useful next decisions.

Capabilities, data connections and the product experience are still being explored. No integrations, automated diagnoses or release date are being offered today.

02

What we are exploring.

Business context & evidence

Capture the business question, segment and time period; connect the signals and qualitative context needed to investigate it.

Signals & competing explanations

Assess changes across the growth system and make alternative explanations visible, including gaps in the evidence.

Prioritised investigations

Compare possible next actions by evidence strength, potential relevance and effort. Keep uncertainty visible rather than presenting every recommendation as certain.

Consultant review & executive reporting

The planned workflow includes human review and a report connecting observations, supporting evidence, open questions and recommended next actions.
