The EdTech SaaS Growth Problem

Why EdTech SaaS companies leave revenue on the table.

These aren't symptoms. They're root-cause GTM failures that compound every quarter.

01
Budget Cycle Dependency
Education buyers run on annual budget cycles. If you're not in their plan by Q1, you wait a year. Your GTM needs to build pipeline 6 months ahead of the decision.
02
The Free Trial Trap
EdTech buyers love free pilots — and then don't pay. Without a clear value demonstration sequence, pilots don't convert to paid.
03
Outcome Measurement Gap
Procurement and leadership buyers demand outcome data. If you can't quantify learning impact, you lose the renewal — even if teachers love the product.
