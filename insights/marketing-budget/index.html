Leadership · Perspective

What SaaS leaders should assess before increasing marketing budgets

More spend is a decision about a system, not just a campaign.

Check the commercial assumptions

Confirm the segment, buying context, offer and conversion path. If these assumptions are weak, extra budget can amplify uncertainty rather than resolve it.

Assess capacity and measurement

Review whether sales can handle additional demand and whether the team can distinguish relevant outcomes from activity. Clarify data limits before treating a report as a complete explanation.

Choose a focused next investment

Specify the hypothesis, resources, decision owner and review point. Increasing budget may be appropriate, but so may improving the offer, fixing a handoff or collecting missing customer evidence.

A general strategic perspective. Your business context should determine the assessment and next steps.
