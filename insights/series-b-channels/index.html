Series B SaaS growth: when more channels create less clarity

Channel expansion can multiply activity faster than understanding.

Inspect the portfolio of activity

List the audience, purpose, investment and learning objective of each channel. A channel without a clear role can consume attention without improving commercial understanding.

Look for overlap and handoff gaps

Different teams may target the same accounts with incompatible messages. Review the customer journey as a whole, including how interest reaches sales and how feedback returns to marketing.

Expand with a decision rule

Define what success, failure and sufficient evidence look like for a channel. Resourcing decisions should reflect your context and learning, not an assumption that more channels mean more growth.

A general strategic perspective. Your business context should determine the assessment and next steps.
