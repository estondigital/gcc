Product marketing · Perspective

How product positioning influences revenue growth

Clear positioning helps the right buyer understand why the product matters.

Make the comparison explicit

Customers evaluate a product against their current approach and other options. Positioning should explain the relevant problem, the buyer and the difference that matters in that comparison.

Connect the narrative to execution

A positioning statement alone does not change the buying journey. Reflect it in product pages, campaigns, sales conversations and onboarding expectations.

Use evidence rather than slogans

Interview customers and prospects, review objections and examine the contexts where the product fits. Stronger positioning should be assessed through relevant customer and commercial signals, not assumed to guarantee revenue.
