Demand generation · Perspective

Why more leads do not always mean healthier growth

Volume matters only in the context of fit, conversion and commercial value.

Define a useful lead

A content download and an active buying enquiry represent different intent. Establish what each signal means before comparing channels or reporting progress.

Follow the journey beyond acquisition

Review qualification, sales acceptance, conversion, customer value and retention context. More acquisition can add cost if the downstream journey cannot turn relevant interest into durable customer value.

Align the learning loop

Marketing and sales should agree on useful feedback: which conversations progress, where expectations diverge and which segments show meaningful fit. Use that evidence to improve targeting and messaging.

A general strategic perspective. Your business context should determine the assessment and next steps.
