Common Questions

What people ask before booking.

Is this really free?
Yes. The Revenue Growth Audit is a 45-minute conversation at no charge. The goal is to diagnose your GTM system and determine whether a paid engagement makes sense for both sides. If it doesn't, you still leave with useful clarity.
Who is this for exactly?
Founders, CEOs, and heads of marketing or growth at B2B SaaS companies between $500K and $20M ARR. If your pipeline is inconsistent, your CAC is rising, or your GTM feels misaligned — this conversation is built for you.
What happens after I submit the form?
Jithin reviews every submission personally within 24 hours. If there's a clear fit, you'll receive a calendar link for a 45-minute video call. We'll also send a short pre-call brief so the conversation is focused from minute one.
How is this different from a free strategy call?
Most "free strategy calls" are discovery calls in disguise — they learn about you and pitch a service. This audit is structured around your specific GTM data: ARR, CAC, win rate, sales cycle, churn, ICP clarity. You're getting a real diagnosis, not a pitch.
What if I'm pre-revenue or very early stage?
The audit is most valuable at $500K ARR and above, where there's enough GTM signal to diagnose. If you're earlier, the Growth Scorecard tool is a better starting point — it takes 3 minutes and gives you an immediate assessment.
What does a typical engagement look like after the audit?
Engagements vary by stage and need — from a focused 4-week positioning sprint to a 3-month full GTM system build. Scope and pricing are discussed openly after the audit. There's no standard package — everything is built around your specific constraint.
