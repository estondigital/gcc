02

One system. Different decisions.

Founders & CEOs

Benefit: a clearer basis for deciding where to focus leadership time and budget. Connect the growth symptom to the evidence, understand the trade-offs and give your team a shared next priority.

Marketing, revenue & growth leaders

Benefit: a better understanding of which audiences, messages and handoffs deserve attention. Separate activity from qualified demand and define a focused investigation before committing more budget.

RevOps & growth operations

Benefit: a shared measurement language across teams. Identify conflicting definitions, missing evidence and unreliable handoffs so leadership discussions start from comparable information.

Product & customer-success leaders

Benefit: a clearer connection between the acquisition promise and customer value. Investigate where onboarding, adoption or renewal breaks down and align teams around the next customer problem to examine.

03

What you can take into your next decision.

A focused growth question

Replace a broad concern such as “growth is slowing” with a specific question about a segment, stage or customer journey.

An evidence checklist

Know which metrics, customer perspectives and context are needed before choosing an explanation. Make missing evidence visible.

Prioritised next actions

Compare possible investigations by relevance, evidence strength and effort. Agree an owner and a review point rather than creating an unranked list of ideas.

A clearer leadership conversation

Bring observations, uncertainty and options together in a decision brief. Consulting scope and outputs are agreed for your engagement; business outcomes are not guaranteed.
