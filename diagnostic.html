Set up Google Analytics first — it's free, and it takes about 15 minutes.

Without analytics you are making go-to-market decisions blind. You cannot see which channels bring buyers, where the funnel leaks, or whether a change helped. For a B2B SaaS company between $1M and $20M ARR this is the single cheapest fix available — and it is a prerequisite for almost every other improvement in your report.

  1. Go to analytics.google.com and create a free account.
  2. Create a GA4 property for your website and copy the measurement ID.
  3. Add the tag to your site — directly, or through Google Tag Manager.
  4. Mark your key events as conversions: demo request, trial signup, pricing view, contact form.
  5. Also install Microsoft Clarity — also free — for heatmaps and session recordings.
  6. Let it collect for 28 days, then come back and re-run this diagnostic with real data attached.

Your report will still generate now from your questionnaire answers alone — it will simply be scored at self-reported confidence, and RevOps & data will reflect the missing measurement layer.
