SaaS Growth Bottleneck Map

Map the symptom before choosing the solution.

Use this framework in a team discussion. It is an investigation aid, not a validated diagnostic model.

01

A closer look.

Acquisition → Qualified demand

Compare traffic sources, target-account fit and intent. Ask whether new volume reaches the right audience.

Conversion → Revenue

Inspect opportunity cohorts, qualification, objections, sales cycle and reasons for loss.

Activation → Value

Define the first meaningful value event. Compare completion by persona, source and onboarding path.

Retention → Durable value

Separate customer and revenue retention. Review product adoption, churn interviews and customer cohorts.

Efficiency → Sustainable economics

Align acquisition costs, payback, gross margin and expansion by cohort before interpreting averages.
