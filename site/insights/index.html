A closer look.

When more traffic does not mean more opportunity

Start by comparing qualified pipeline per source, not aggregate visits. Check whether the audience mix or qualification definition changed. Then inspect landing-page intent and sales handoffs.

Investigating weak activation

Choose a meaningful value milestone before measuring onboarding. Segment time-to-value and completion by persona. Pair event data with interviews to understand friction.

Why averages hide growth constraints

A company-wide rate can conceal very different cohorts. Compare like-for-like segments and account for time lags before making an executive recommendation.
