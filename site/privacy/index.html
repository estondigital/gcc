A closer look.

Customer research submissions

The research page embeds a form hosted by Tally. Information you submit is processed through Tally and made available to the form owner for reviewing research interest and contacting participants. The form asks about your professional role, company and growth challenges. Please do not include confidential customer or business data.

Research and product updates

Research contact consent covers reviewing your application and contacting you about research. Product-update consent should be a separate, optional choice. Requests for anonymised findings do not imply a platform launch date or product access.

Consulting and platform-interest forms

These preview forms validate details and prepare an email draft. They do not send or store enquiries on the website. You choose whether to send the draft from your email application.

Analytics and your choice

Google Analytics measurement ID G-28TWC9G9TJ measures page use and conversion events only when analytics is accepted. Saved choices are applied on later pages. We do not send names, email addresses, form answers or submission identifiers to Google Analytics. Change your choice using Analytics preferences in the footer.

Campaign attribution

Recognised campaign labels, such as utm_source and utm_campaign, can be carried to the research form to understand how participants found the invitation. Tally must have matching hidden fields to store these labels with responses. Do not put personal or confidential information in campaign URLs.

External services

Google Fonts serves the website fonts. Google Analytics is used with your analytics choice. The research embed contacts Tally to display and process the form, independently of optional analytics. These providers may receive connection information. Read Tally’s privacy information before submitting.
