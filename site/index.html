A few things worth clarifying.

How much does Growth-as-a-Service typically cost?

There is no single price that fits every engagement. Scope, specialist involvement, delivery responsibilities and the level of ongoing support affect cost. Growth Clarity Co. agrees a consulting scope and fee after understanding your priorities; we have not published standard GaaS package prices. Contact us to discuss your needs.

What is the difference between GaaS and hiring a growth team?

Growth as a Service gives you external expertise for an agreed scope, while an internal team provides dedicated day-to-day ownership and company context. At Growth Clarity Co., the starting point is a defined growth question, evidence review and prioritised next actions. Execution responsibilities should be agreed explicitly.

What should you look for in a GaaS provider?

Look for relevant SaaS experience, a clear investigation method, transparent scope and fees, evidence-backed recommendations and agreed ownership of actions. Ask how the provider handles uncertainty and measures progress. Review real work samples and references when available, and be cautious about guaranteed growth claims.

Can GaaS replace a marketing team entirely?

It can support specific capability gaps, but you should not assume it replaces every marketing function. Your team still needs ownership of customer relationships, priorities and implementation. Growth Clarity Co. is a startup offering scoped consulting today while developing its platform; a complete outsourced marketing department is not being promised.
