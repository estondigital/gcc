Partnership enquiries

Connect expertise around a shared growth problem.

We welcome focused conversations with SaaS communities, investor operating teams, specialist advisers and research collaborators who help B2B SaaS teams make better decisions.

01

Where a collaboration could be useful.

SaaS communities & founder networks

Help relevant founders and operators discover voluntary research conversations or practical diagnostic resources. Start with a shared audience and a useful learning question.

Investor & accelerator operating teams

Explore a focused discussion around recurring portfolio growth questions. Any workshop, research collaboration or advisory scope would be agreed separately.

Specialist advisers & operators

Discuss complementary expertise in GTM, product marketing, RevOps, activation or retention. Clarify responsibilities and customer permissions before considering joint work.

Research & education collaborators

Explore evidence-led content or a shared research question. Agree methodology, attribution and participant consent before collecting or publishing findings.

02

Start with a concrete proposal.

Tell us who you help.

Introduce your organisation, the SaaS audience you serve and the growth problem you see repeatedly. Describe the contribution you could make and the outcome you want to explore.

Agree the scope together.

A useful first discussion establishes fit, roles, effort, data permissions and a next step. An enquiry does not establish a partnership; commercial terms and any collaboration would require separate agreement.
